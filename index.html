About

I study how people perceive the quality of images, video, and immersive media, and how to build models that predict it.

I'm a researcher in computer vision and signal processing at the University of Poitiers. Much of my work focuses on perceptual quality assessment for 360-degree images, where I combine subjective studies with deep learning models that account for how viewers explore a scene. I completed my PhD in 2023 with a thesis on objective and subjective quality assessment of 360-degree images.

More recently, I've been working on point cloud quality, video frame selection, license plate super-resolution, the robustness of visual encoders to distortion, and the detection of AI-generated images.

  • Image and video quality assessment
  • 360-degree and immersive media
  • Point cloud quality
  • Super-resolution
  • AI-generated image detection
  • Deep learning for vision
